Labeled single-line input: cloudSoft fill that turns paper-white on focus with a primary ring. `onChange` passes the value directly.

```jsx
<Field label="Usuario o correo" placeholder="Ej. marcos o tu correo" value={v} onChange={setV} />
<Field label="Contraseña" type="password" placeholder="Mínimo 8 caracteres" />
```

Placeholders use the "Ej. …" pattern. Labels are short nouns in operational Spanish.
